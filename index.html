02

03
Payment

Standard orders use the 25% / 25% / 50% payment structure.

04

Work begins once the required payment has been received.

05
Preview + revisions

06
Final files

Once everything is approved and paid, you'll receive your finished files and PSD.

✦ PAYMENT

Standard payment

25% after line art → 25% after flat colors → 50% after I show you a picture of the finished body sheet.

Payment plans

Payment plans are available if you need one. You can pay what you are able to each week until the full price is paid.

The finished bodysheet and final files will not be delivered until the full price has been paid. If you need an extension, communicate with me before your payment deadline.

Payment-processing fees are included in the listed prices.

✦ TURNAROUND

Estimated turnaround is 1–2 weeks. It may take longer depending on workload, health, character complexity, or unexpected delays.

Rush commissions are not available.
