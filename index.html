TERMS OF SERVICE

1. PAYMENT

Standard payment is split into 25% after line art, 25% after flat colors, and 50% after a picture of the finished body sheet.

Payment plans are available for people who need them. Pay what you can each week until the full price is paid.

The finished sheet will not be delivered until fully paid unless an extension has been communicated.

Payment-processing fees are included in the listed price.

2. CANCELLATION / REFUNDS

If the commission has just started and no sketch has been sent, no money has been paid yet.

If cancellation happens after the sketch: full refund.

If cancellation happens after lineart and flat colors: 50% refund.

If the commission is almost finished: no refund.

3. ARTIST CANCELLATION

PuppiPyro may cancel a commission when necessary.

If the artist cancels because the commission cannot be finished or the artist simply cannot continue, the customer will receive a refund appropriate to the circumstances.

If the customer is disrespectful, rude, or violates the TOS, a refund may not be provided.

4. ABANDONMENT

After the commission form: approximately 48 hours without response before cancellation may occur.

After the sketch is sent: approximately 4 days.

After lineart and flat colors: approximately 2 days.

After the finished render: approximately 24 hours.

5. REVISIONS

Around 10 revisions are free. After approximately 10 revisions, additional revisions cost $0.50 each.

6. TURNAROUND

Typical turnaround is 1–2 weeks. It may take longer because of illness, workload, complexity, or other delays.

Rush orders are not accepted.

7. WILL DRAW

  • Human characters
  • Humanoid characters
  • Male characters
  • Female characters
  • Two-legged furry characters
  • Gacha characters
  • Stylized characters
  • Custom outfits
  • Reasonably complex designs

8. WON'T DRAW

  • Mecha
  • Heavy armor
  • Extremely complex designs
  • Four-legged characters
  • Explicit NSFW
  • Extreme gore
  • Copied designs
  • Severe eyestrain designs
  • AI-generated references

9. COMMERCIAL USE

Customers may use their character and commissioned body sheet in videos. Monetized videos are allowed.

Credit to PuppiPyro is required.

The commissioned artwork itself may not be resold.

10. PSD / EDITING

The PSD is provided after final payment. Layers are separated for editing and tweening.

Customers may edit the PSD for their character and intended use, but may not claim the original artwork was created by them.

11. CREDIT

Credit to PuppiPyro is required. A link back is preferred when possible.

12. POSTING / PRIVACY

PuppiPyro may post completed commissions as portfolio examples unless privacy has been requested.

Customers may request that their commission not be publicly displayed.

13. AI REFERENCES

AI-generated character references are not accepted.

14. CUSTOMER RESPONSIBILITY

Customers are responsible for providing accurate references, character information, and instructions.

Commission requests must go through the official form and should not be used to bypass the queue.

FAQ

How do I commission you?

Fill out the commission form. Your submission will be reviewed and placed into the queue or waitlist.

Payment is handled through Ko-fi.

How long does it take?

Usually 1–2 weeks, but it may take longer depending on circumstances.

Can I commission multiple characters?

Yes, but multiple characters affect your position in the queue.

Do you draw furry characters?

Yes, as long as they are two-legged.

Do you draw males?

Yes.

Can I use AI references?

No. AI-generated references are not accepted.

Can I use my commission commercially?

Yes. You may use your character and commissioned bodysheet in videos, including monetized videos. Credit is required.

Can I request changes?

Yes. Around 10 revisions are free. Additional revisions after that are $0.50 each.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds depend on how far the commission has progressed. See the full TOS for the exact cancellation policy.

No.

How do I join the waitlist?

Submit the commission form. If active slots are full, eligible submissions are placed onto the waitlist.

Can I get both front and side?

Yes. Front and side versions are available. When ordered together for the same character, they can count as one character slot.

Do I get the PSD?

Yes. The PSD is provided after final payment requirements are completed.

Can I ask questions through Discord?

General questions can be asked through your preferred contact method, but commission submissions should go through the official form.
