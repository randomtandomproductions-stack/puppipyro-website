PAYMENT

Standard payment is:

25% → 25% → 50%

25% after lineart

25% after flat colors

PAYMENT PLANS

Payment plans are available for people who need them.

You can pay what you are able to each week until the full price is paid.

Finished files will not be delivered until the commission is fully paid.

If you need more time, communicate with me about an extension.

Payment-processing fees are already included in the listed price.

I WILL DRAW

  • Gacha characters
  • Stylized designs
  • Custom outfits

I WON'T DRAW

  • Mecha
  • Heavy armor
  • Extremely complex designs
  • Four-legged characters
  • Explicit NSFW

  • Copied designs
  • Severe eyestrain
  • AI references

REVISIONS

Around 10 revisions are free. After about 10 revisions, additional revisions cost $0.50 each.

COMMERCIAL USE

You may use your commissioned character and body sheet in videos and other monetized content.
